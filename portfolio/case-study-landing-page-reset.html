Overview

The Landing Page & Website Refresh was approached as a strategic website initiative rather than a visual redesign. The client, an NDA-protected physical therapy brand, had an existing website that provided a solid foundation, but there was an opportunity to make the experience clearer, more intuitive, and more directly connected to the brand's marketing efforts.

The goal was to improve how visitors move through the website, understand the brand's services, find relevant information, and take the next step — whether that meant booking a call, submitting a form, or claiming an offer. New landing pages were also built from scratch to support newly launched physical therapy services, while the broader site was refreshed to bring structure, content, and conversion pathways into alignment.

The Challenge

The existing digital experience provided a foundation, but there was an opportunity to make the website clearer, more intuitive, and more conversion-focused. As the brand introduced new physical therapy services and leaned further into digital marketing campaigns, the website needed to keep pace with that growth.

Strategic Question

“How can the website and its landing pages be refreshed so that every visitor understands the offer, trusts the brand, and knows exactly what to do next?”

  • Information could be organized more clearly across the site
  • Landing page structure could be strengthened to support new services
  • Important information needed stronger visual hierarchy
  • Calls-to-action could be more prominent and consistent
  • The user journey could be simplified from arrival to action
  • Content could be made easier to scan on desktop and mobile
  • Website sections needed better alignment with visitor intent
  • The site needed to better support ongoing marketing campaigns
  • Landing pages needed a stronger connection between content and conversion

Strategy

The website was treated as part of a larger digital marketing ecosystem rather than an isolated design project. Every page needed to work alongside social, email, and seasonal campaigns rather than exist as a separate destination visitors landed on and left.

The Strategic Framework

Clarity → User Experience → Trust → Action

The refresh was designed to help every visitor:

  • Understand what the brand offers
  • Identify relevant information quickly
  • Build trust in the brand and its services
  • Find the next step without confusion
  • Take action with minimal friction

Balancing user needs, brand positioning, content, SEO, conversion, and visual presentation meant no single discipline was prioritized at the expense of another — the website needed to look credible, read clearly, rank reasonably, and convert consistently.

05

Landing Page Strategy

New landing pages were built from scratch to support newly launched physical therapy services, alongside a refresh of existing pages. Each landing page was designed around a clear above-the-fold experience and a logical content progression.

Above-the-Fold Experience

The top section of each landing page was designed to immediately communicate who the brand is, what it offers, who it serves, why the visitor should care, and what action to take next — without requiring any scrolling to understand the offer.

Content Hierarchy

Headline → Value Proposition → Supporting Information
↓
Benefits / Services → Social Proof / Trust → CTA
↓
Additional Information → Final CTA

CTA Strategy

The previous CTA experience relied on an outdated layout with a messy overall flow, which made the intended next step less obvious. CTAs were rebuilt around clear, consistent placement and wording tied to the primary conversion actions: booking a call, submitting a form, or claiming a freebie/offer.

  • Clear CTA placement at logical points in the page
  • Action-oriented CTA wording matched to visitor intent
  • A single primary action per page, with secondary actions supporting it
  • Reduced decision friction by limiting competing choices
  • Important CTAs repeated at logical points further down the page
Intended Journey

Landing Page → CTA → Next Step → Conversion

06

Website Refresh & Content Architecture

Beyond individual landing pages, the broader website was refreshed to function as a more useful marketing asset. Work spanned the homepage, navigation, header and footer, service pages, forms, and internal linking structure.

  • Reorganized page structure and navigation for easier wayfinding
  • Refreshed homepage and key service pages
  • Rebuilt header and footer structure for consistency across pages
  • Clarified content hierarchy so key information surfaces sooner
  • Strengthened contact and booking pathways
  • Improved internal linking between related pages and content
  • Aligned CTAs across the site rather than treating each page in isolation

The result was a website that felt more cohesive from page to page, with a consistent structure that made it easier for visitors to navigate and easier for the brand to maintain going forward.

07

Content & Copy Optimization

The client provided existing website copy, which served as the foundation for new content proposals. Rather than rewriting copy for aesthetics alone, each section was restructured to answer three questions:

Guiding Questions

What does the visitor need to know? Why does it matter? What should they do next?

  • Refined headlines and subheadings for clarity and relevance
  • Rebuilt value propositions around what visitors actually care about
  • Restructured paragraphs for scannability rather than dense text blocks
  • Rewrote CTA copy to be specific and action-oriented
  • Shifted messaging toward benefits rather than feature lists
  • Incorporated search-friendly wording without sacrificing readability
08

SEO & Discoverability

SEO was built into the refresh rather than treated as a separate, after-the-fact task. Work focused on keyword relevance, on-page optimization, local SEO setup, and technical/content discoverability.

  • Researched search terms relevant to how local clients look for physical therapy services
  • Added relevant keyphrases naturally into page titles, headings, and image descriptions
  • Reviewed and updated the Google Business Profile with accurate hours, location details, and updated photos
  • Improved mobile page-loading speed so visitors could access information quickly
  • Restructured headings for clearer search intent alignment
  • Strengthened internal linking between service pages and supporting content

Mobile & Responsive Experience

Mobile optimization was part of the refresh from the start. Since a meaningful share of visitors arrive from social and email campaigns on their phones, the mobile experience needed to hold up on its own — not simply function as a scaled-down version of the desktop site.

  • Reviewed and adjusted layout and section stacking for mobile screens
  • Ensured CTAs remained visible and reachable without excessive scrolling
  • Improved text readability and spacing on smaller screens
  • Resized and optimized images for faster mobile loading
  • Simplified mobile navigation for easier one-handed use

Mobile responsiveness mattered here specifically because campaign traffic — social, email, and seasonal promotions — tends to land on mobile first, and any friction at that stage directly affects conversion.

10

Conversion Strategy

The refresh supported the full path from visitor to action, not just the landing page moment. This meant thinking about where traffic came from, what visitors needed to see, and how to reduce friction at every step.

Traffic → Landing Page → Information → Trust → CTA → Conversion
  • Clear, consistent CTAs across pages and campaigns
  • Strategic CTA placement based on content hierarchy
  • Trust-building content placed ahead of the primary ask
  • Reduced friction between interest and action
  • Consistent messaging between campaigns and landing destinations

The website was designed to support the broader marketing funnel — social media, email, and seasonal campaigns feeding into landing pages built to convert rather than simply inform.

11

Execution

The refresh combined strategy and hands-on execution across the full Wix build. Work was completed over a four-week core sprint, with ongoing maintenance and updates continuing through the length of the engagement.

  • Full website audit across UX, content, conversion, design, and SEO
  • Landing page planning and wireframing for new physical therapy services
  • Built new landing pages from scratch on Wix
  • Refreshed existing landing pages and key website sections
  • Website content restructuring and navigation improvements
  • Copy refinement based on existing client-provided content
  • CTA rebuild and optimization across the site
  • Visual design created in Canva as requested by the client
  • SEO optimization: titles, headings, alt text, and internal linking
  • Google Business Profile updates as part of local SEO
  • Image replacement and optimization for faster loading
  • Mobile optimization and responsive testing
  • Quality assurance and ongoing performance monitoring

Strategic Impact

This project demonstrates the ability to combine digital marketing, website strategy, landing page optimization, UX thinking, content strategy, conversion optimization, SEO, CTA strategy, website management, analytics, and marketing funnel thinking into a single, cohesive engagement.

Core Takeaway

A website refresh is not just a design exercise. It is an opportunity to improve how content, user experience, search visibility, and conversion work together.

16

Key Takeaways

01

Website design should support business and marketing objectives, not exist as a separate, disconnected effort.

02

Clear content hierarchy reduces friction for users and makes the intended next step obvious.

03

Landing pages should guide visitors toward one specific action rather than presenting too many competing choices.

04

SEO should be considered during content and website development, not added afterward as an afterthought.

05

Small, deliberate improvements to structure, messaging, and CTAs can create measurable changes in user behavior.
